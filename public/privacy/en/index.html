Duetrack privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Versione italiana

Duetrack is a web application that reads work sessions from a Google Calendar chosen by the user and works out how much each of their clients owes. This page explains which data it uses, where that data is kept and how to delete it.

In short

  • Duetrack has no server: it runs entirely in your browser.
  • Your data stays on your device and in your own Google Drive. The developer of Duetrack never sees or receives it.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies.

Google user data Duetrack accesses

Duetrack accesses only the Google data listed below, and only after you give consent on Google’s consent screen. These are the narrowest permissions available.

  • The list of your calendars, read-only (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). Data read: the name and colour of your calendars. Why: so you can pick the calendar where you record your sessions. Where it stays: in memory only, to show the list.
  • Events of the calendars you own, read-only (calendar.events.owned.readonly). Data read: title, start and end date and time of the events in the calendar you choose. Why: to recognise the client (the title is their name) and compute duration and amount from their rate. Where it stays: events are read in your browser and kept in memory while the app is open; they are not copied to your Drive or to any server. Duetrack never modifies your calendar.
  • Files created by Duetrack in your Google Drive (drive.file). Data read and written: a single file, duetrack-data.json, holding your clients, rates, payments and manual corrections. Why: to keep your devices in sync without a server. Duetrack cannot see or open any other file in your Drive.

Duetrack does not access your email, contacts, photos or any other data in your Google account. Data you receive from Google is not shared with anyone: it is not sold, not passed to third parties and not used for advertising.

Where the data is kept

  • In your browser (IndexedDB): the app data, so it opens instantly and works offline.
  • In your Google Drive: the file duetrack-data.json, which you own.
  • The Google access token is kept in memory only while the app is open and expires after about an hour. It is never stored anywhere.

Requests go straight from your browser to Google. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any hosting service, may log technical data about visits (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy.

How we protect the data

Sensitive data, meaning your clients, the amounts they owe and your work sessions, is protected as follows:

  • No server of ours. Duetrack has no backend and no database: your data never passes through a server run by the developer, so nobody but you can read it.
  • Encryption in transit. All communication between your browser, the app and Google’s APIs uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Access token in memory only. The permission issued by Google is never saved to disk, to the browser or to Drive, it expires after about an hour and there are no refresh tokens. Closing the tab discards it.
  • Minimum permissions. The calendar is read-only and, on Drive, the app sees only the file it created. Even in case of a bug, Duetrack could not read your other files or modify your events.
  • Your account’s protection. The Drive file is yours and stays protected by your Google account’s security (for example two-step verification). The copy in your browser is protected by the profile of the device you use: we recommend locking it with a password or PIN.
  • No human access. The developer has no technical way to see your data and does not read it. The source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check this.
  • No sharing. Data is not sold, transferred, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train artificial intelligence models.

How long data is kept

Duetrack keeps nothing on its own. Your data stays on your device and in the file in your Drive until you delete it, using the steps below.

Use of data received from Google APIs

Duetrack’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to show you your own sessions and figures: it is not sold, not used for advertising and not transferred to third parties. No human reads it.

How to delete your data

  • Delete the file duetrack-data.json from your Google Drive.
  • Clear the site data of duetrack.federicodiluca.com in your browser settings.
  • Revoke Duetrack’s access to your account on the Third-party apps with account access page.
